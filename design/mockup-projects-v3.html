    What's new in v3

    Full-page case studies

    Clicking a project now opens a cinematic full-screen page — 82vh hero with slow settle-in, facts bar, editorial chapters with full-bleed images between them, gallery, and a "Next Project" teaser that keeps you browsing. No more boxed modal.

    The uniform 2-column grid became a 12-column staggered composition — alternating widths, portrait/landscape crops, and vertical offsets, with index numbers (01, 02…). Reflows to a clean single column on mobile.

    Sector filters become a sticky, swipeable row under the nav; case-study pages scroll natively full-screen; gallery goes 2-up; lightbox arrows enlarged; all tap targets ≥44px.

    Motion, choreographed

    One Ken Burns settle on the hero, scroll-triggered reveals, slow 1.2s image zooms — all respecting reduced-motion preferences. No scattered effects.
