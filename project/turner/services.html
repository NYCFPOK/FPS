Scope of Work

Our in-house capabilities span the full spectrum of construction trades — from structural and mechanical systems to architectural finishes and specialty installations.

Alarm & Sprinkler Systems
Full life-safety system installation, testing, and compliance to NYC code requirements.
Electrical & Plumbing Systems
Complete electrical service, distribution, lighting, and all plumbing rough-in and finish work.
Elevator Construction
New elevator installation and cab modernization in occupied and new-construction buildings.
Floor & Wall Finishes
Tile, hardwood, carpet, VCT, paint, wallcovering, and specialty finish systems.
Handicapped Access Construction
ADA-compliant ramps, restrooms, doorways, and accessible routes throughout a facility.
Heating & Cooling Systems
HVAC installation and replacement, including ductwork, VAV systems, and rooftop units.
Lighting Environments
Architectural, task, and ambient lighting design and installation for any environment.
Masonry
Brick, block, stone, concrete, and masonry restoration by experienced tradespeople.
Renovation & New Construction
Full interior and structural renovations as well as ground-up new construction projects.
Roof Replacement
Complete flat and pitched roofing systems, insulation, drainage, and waterproofing.
Windows & Doors
Storefront, curtain wall, commercial windows, hollow metal, and architectural wood doors.
