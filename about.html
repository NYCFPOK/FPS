Our Story

Built on craftsmanship.
Driven by integrity.

FPS Contracting Inc. was founded in Maspeth, Queens, and has grown into one of New York's most trusted general contracting firms. For over 30 years, we have served the metropolitan area with a commitment to quality workmanship, honest communication, and on-time delivery.

Our expertise spans healthcare facilities, institutional buildings, commercial interiors, supportive and affordable housing, luxury residential renovations, and real estate development. We are fully licensed, bonded, and insured, operating under the highest standards of safety and compliance.

At FPS, we believe that great construction is about more than materials and methods — it's about the relationships we build with clients, architects, and communities. Many of our clients have worked with us for 15 to 25 years, a testament to the trust we earn on every project.

Our Mission

Building environments that empower people.

Our mission is to serve the greater metropolitan area as a premier general contractor, delivering exceptional construction, development, and property management services with integrity, precision, and care.

We specialize in institutional, financial, and healthcare projects, as well as supportive housing, residential development, and real estate management — creating safe, sustainable, and functional spaces that serve communities and support long-term growth. Through expert craftsmanship, collaborative partnerships, and a commitment to excellence, we build environments that empower people and elevate standards across the sectors we serve.

The Team Behind
Every Project

FPS Contracting is a family-owned and operated firm. The Alesci family has built the company from the ground up, combining decades of hands-on construction experience with a deep commitment to client relationships.

FA
Francesco Alesci
President & Founder

Known as "Frank," Francesco Alesci brings more than 40 years of general contracting experience and a proven ability to lead complex projects from planning through completion. As President and Founder of FPS Contracting Inc., he guides company strategy, client relationships, and project execution with a hands-on understanding of construction, scheduling, coordination, and problem solving. His leadership has helped establish FPS as a trusted New York contractor for healthcare, institutional, commercial, supportive housing, and development-driven work.

PA
Peter Alesci
Vice President, Field Operations

Peter Alesci joined FPS Contracting in 2000 and has become a key leader in the company's field operations. As Vice President of Field Operations, he oversees active construction sites with a strong focus on scheduling, coordination, quality control, and day-to-day execution. His hands-on leadership helps keep projects moving efficiently while maintaining the workmanship, organization, and accountability that FPS clients expect.

SA
Salvatore Alesci
Owner & Director

Salvatore Alesci joined FPS Contracting in 2000 and plays an important leadership role in the company's continued growth and operations. As Owner and Director, he helps oversee client relationships, project planning, internal coordination, and business development across FPS Contracting's institutional and commercial construction markets. His attention to detail and commitment to service help ensure every project is delivered with professionalism, clarity, and the high standards that define FPS.
