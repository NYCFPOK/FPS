Full-Service General Contractor

Full-Service Construction
& Development.

FPS Contracting is a full-service general contractor providing construction management, preconstruction, development support, consulting, infrastructure upgrades, renovations, and commercial build-outs. We work with property owners, developers, institutions, facility managers, and commercial clients to deliver organized, well-managed projects from early planning through completion. Our approach is built on clear communication, practical field experience, dependable coordination, and a commitment to professionalism and accountability.

Our Approach

How We Deliver

01

Construction Management

FPS manages construction projects with a hands-on approach to planning, scheduling, budgeting, coordination, and field execution. We work closely with clients, design professionals, subcontractors, and all project stakeholders to keep each phase moving efficiently.

  • Estimated Cost & Budget Development
  • Project Planning & Scheduling
  • Job Meetings & Progress Updates
  • Trade Coordination & Site Supervision
  • Permit, Plan & Certificate of Occupancy Coordination
  • Quality Control & Project Closeout
02

General Contracting

As a general contractor, FPS provides complete project execution for commercial, institutional, and residential construction. We coordinate the trades, manage the site, oversee the schedule, and ensure work is completed to scope, budget, and quality expectations.

  • Commercial & Office Build-Outs
  • Healthcare & Medical Facilities
  • Nursing Homes & Care Facilities
  • Supportive & Affordable Housing
  • Government & Institutional
  • Renovations & New Construction
03

Preconstruction Services

Successful projects begin long before construction starts. FPS provides preconstruction support to help clients understand the full scope, budget, schedule, logistics, and potential challenges before work begins in the field.

  • Conceptual Budgeting & Scope Development
  • Preliminary Scheduling
  • Constructability Review
  • Value Engineering
  • Site Condition Review
  • Trade Pricing & Bid Coordination
04

Development & Consulting Support

FPS supports developers, property owners, and commercial clients with practical construction guidance throughout the development process — from early feasibility through construction execution, protecting budget, timeline, and long-term value.

  • Early Project Planning
  • Site & Building Condition Review
  • Capital Improvement Planning
  • Owner Advisory Support
  • Budget & Schedule Review
  • Renovation & Adaptive Reuse Support
Scope of Work

Infrastructure & Building Improvements

FPS performs and coordinates building upgrades that improve safety, performance, accessibility, and long-term functionality — working efficiently in active environments and coordinating around daily operations when needed.

Electrical & Plumbing Systems
Complete electrical service, distribution, lighting, and all plumbing rough-in and finish work.
Heating & Cooling (HVAC)
HVAC system installation, coordination, and replacement in occupied and new-construction environments.
Fire Alarm & Sprinkler Systems
Full life-safety system installation, testing, and compliance to NYC code requirements.
Elevator Construction
New elevator installation and cab modernization in occupied and new-construction buildings.
Accessibility & ADA Improvements
Ramps, accessible bathrooms, door widening, compliant fixtures, and ADA-related construction.
Roofing & Waterproofing
Roof replacement, coating, membrane, flashing, parapets, and building envelope restoration.
Masonry Repairs & Upgrades
Brick repair, pointing, facade work, and structural masonry improvements.
Windows & Doors
Replacement windows, curtainwall, storefront systems, and door upgrades.
Lighting Systems
Interior and exterior lighting environments, LED upgrades, and specialty lighting installations.
Floor & Wall Finishes
Tile, hardwood, carpet, VCT, paint, wallcovering, and specialty architectural finish systems.
